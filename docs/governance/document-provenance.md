## Reference-change and recovery controls

Record a source checkpoint and collision-free location map before moving files. Rebase links and executable paths, preserve stable IDs, distinguish immutable historical hashes from current reference revisions, and run both positive validation and mutation regressions. Remove old active locations and temporary tooling only after their replacements and necessary evidence are reconciled.

Checksums detect drift against a recorded value; they do not independently authenticate a file or its manifest. Protected main, actual reviews appropriate to risk, explicit owner decisions and exact-head hosted CI remain separate controls. A rejected push is not permission to bypass protection or fabricate a check.

No historical originals, Git history, private field records or existing Lovable project are deleted or recreated by this cleanup. No application, permanent brand approval or new phase closure is claimed. Current verification status is recorded by the lineage register and delivery review; pending reconciliation must not be labeled complete.
