## Purpose

Define the product intent, boundaries, principles, and approval gates for a multi-channel local-services marketplace beginning in Tanuku, Andhra Pradesh. This charter authorizes planning only; it does not authorize implementation, Lovable work, external integrations, spending, or release.

## Problem and opportunity

Customers need a low-friction way to find eligible local service providers, request immediate or scheduled help, and understand request progress. Merchants need a free-registration channel for eligible demand and a reliable way to receive and manage work. Operations need one authoritative system for verification, dispatch, support, auditability, and safety.

The problem statement is a product hypothesis requiring validation with customers, merchants, operations, legal advisers, WhatsApp, and app-store requirements. No interviews, market measurements, or demand evidence were supplied.

## Product vision

Provide a trustworthy, bilingual local-services coordination service that helps customers request work and helps verified merchants respond, while keeping assignment, capacity, privacy, and operational decisions authoritative in the backend.

## Initial market and expansion

- Initial target geography: Tanuku, Andhra Pradesh, India.
- Planned expansion candidates: Bhimavaram and Eluru, then other Indian cities.
- Expansion rule: each city must pass evidence gates defined in a later phase; expansion is not approved by this charter.

## Principles

1. Truthful status: never fabricate price, availability, rating, or arrival time.
2. Backend authority: all channels use one authoritative backend.
3. Fair dispatch: the first valid backend-committed acceptance wins.
4. Free basic merchant participation: registration and basic listings do not require payment.
5. Privacy and least privilege by default.
6. One coordinated public production release after internal gates, not incremental public launches.
7. Evidence before lock-in: recommendations remain proposed until proof and owner approval exist.
8. Visual brand decisions remain open: no permanent logo, colors, typography, tagline, or domain is selected by this name decision.

## Success definition for planning

Phase 00 succeeds when the nine required documents are internally consistent, requirements have stable IDs, decisions and risks have owners and evidence paths, unresolved external dependencies are visible, and the owner can approve or revise the foundation. Product success, technical readiness, regulatory compliance, and production readiness are not established here.

## Release intent

The product objective is one complete coordinated public production release covering the agreed launch scope. This is a planning constraint, not evidence that the release is feasible or funded. The ₹50,000 allowance is treated as validation funding, not as a guaranteed complete engineering budget.

## Explicit non-authorizations

This charter does not authorize application code, infrastructure, databases, package installation, Lovable initialization, UI screen generation, cloud services, paid services, App Store or Play Store submissions, deployment, repository creation, commits, pushes, or later phases.
