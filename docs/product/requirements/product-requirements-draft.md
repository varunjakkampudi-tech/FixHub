## Product requirements document

Fix Hub, under Viswatra, proposes a Tanuku household-service marketplace connecting customers with eligible independently verified providers. Free basic merchant registration/listing is preserved. One backend owns request identity, eligibility, state and committed assignment. First valid committed acceptance wins; notification delivery or UI tap time does not determine the winner. Website/PWA, official WhatsApp, Android and iOS remain coordinated-release objectives, not approved available channels. No MVP/public staggered-release substitution is introduced.

Five primary personas: customer managing their own request; merchant owner managing one business; independently authenticated technician assigned limited work; operations staff managing authorized cases; Super Admin governing platform policy. Merchant manager and assigned reviewer are scoped capability sets, not general administrator aliases. No person shares the owner's credentials. Candidate technician identity and verification rules remain subject to security/legal review.

Commercial success is NOT ASSESSED. No demand, conversion, availability, attendance, price, revenue, rating or arrival guarantee is inferred. Requirements serve discoverability, clear prices/inspection terms, safe offers, reliable state, explicit consent, bilingual use and accountable support. Metrics distinguish request attempts, unique valid requests, eligible offers, accepted assignments, completed work, cancellations and support incidents. Define observation window, denominator, exclusions and freshness for each metric before use. GTV is not platform revenue.

## Authorization and identity matrix

This refines candidate policy only; it does **not** amend the [existing matrix](../../security/access-control.md). The source matrix currently lists an audited Super Admin emergency assignment override, while its open questions require a separately approved emergency-access policy. This draft proposes no routine ops/admin override and treats any exceptional override as disabled until the authority, scope, dual-control requirements, audit and active-job handling are explicitly reconciled. This is a known policy conflict, not a settled permission change; resolve it in the approved requirements and security policy before implementation.

| Action/resource | Customer | Merchant owner / delegated manager | Technician | Operations / reviewer | Super Admin | Service account / worker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Profile, account and sessions | Own verified subject | Own subject/business grants | Own subject, no shared owner login | Assigned support scope; no credential view | Governed recovery only | No human login; purpose-bound scheduled work only |
| Create/read/cancel request | Own, legal state only | Own offer subset; manager requires explicit action grant | Assigned job subset | Case-scoped on-behalf action with customer authority recorded | Explicit grant/audit | Only explicitly defined scheduled commands; never impersonate a user |
| Offer acceptance | Deny | Current offer + tenant + actor grant + capacity | Deny by default; delegation requires explicit policy | Deny; no routine manual winner override | No override by generic admin grant | No merchant impersonation or winner selection outside the committed dispatch command |
| Verification | Own status if applicant | Submit own evidence, never decide | Limited status | Assigned non-conflicted reviewer only | Governed reviewer grant; no automated competence approval | No evidence approval; only narrowly defined processing tasks |
| Quote/work progression | Confirm own quote | Own job, assigned staff and legal state | Assigned allowed progress, not arbitrary price | Case oversight, no customer consent impersonation | Governed incident actions only | No acting as customer, merchant or technician |
| Grants/configuration/audit | No platform grants | Limited business-member delegation, never platform grants | No grant creation | Named scoped capability, no self-grant | MFA, explicit grant, reason and audited change | No human grant; only audited configured maintenance jobs |
| Privacy export/deletion | Own authenticated request | Own subject and lawful business request | Own subject request | Authorized privacy case, masked evidence | Scoped orchestration; holds and proof of deletion, no arbitrary bulk export | Only purpose-bound, logged processing after authorization; no unrestricted export |

This is a candidate matrix, not an authorization grant. A manager receives no permission unless the exact action/resource grant is approved and active. A service account receives no human identity, general read capability, or unspecified permission merely because it appears in this table. The source [access-control proposal](../../security/access-control.md) remains a planning baseline; its incomplete role-column summary and the emergency-override discrepancy must be reconciled before any final policy is approved.

Authentication mechanism/provider is OPEN. Enrollment must distinguish phone verification from legal identity and competence. Account linking needs explicit authenticated proof of both identities and revocation behavior. Recovery cannot silently transfer merchant or privileged grants. Founder-assisted registration records actual business representative consent; public listing is not permission to create an account.

## Candidate product policies

**Category and pricing:** use the [four-family bounded recommendation](../feasibility/market/service-category-opportunities.md) as disabled taxonomy candidates. Task exclusions, inspection/travel/labour/parts/tax disclosure and quote-expiry policy require owner/legal approval. Show INR and price version; no unknown-as-zero. Customer consents to exact quote revision before work. No platform collection, commission agreement, cancellation fee, refund, insurance or guarantee is authorized. Free basic registration/listing never depends on a paid tier or ranking.

**Owner-supplied payment direction — draft, not a commercial/legal approval (2026-10-16):** defer a payment page and platform payment integration; the current preference is COD. Continue to show merchant prices and the current itemized quote inside the customer experience before consent. Do not represent COD as a finalized settlement, tax, invoice-issuer, dispute or refund policy; those details require owner, legal and tax review before customer-facing commitment. No payment-provider selection or payment implementation is authorized.

**Service record:** provide an authenticated customer with a downloadable bill/receipt for an eligible completed service, if and when the applicable transaction and issuer rules are approved. The document must distinguish merchant-provided charges from any platform charge, preserve the accepted quote/adjustment history, and never invent tax, payment or completion facts. Issuer identity, required fields, correction, retention and tax treatment remain OPEN pending qualified review; this is a draft requirement, not an approved invoice or accounting policy.

**Instant/scheduled:** instant searches use currently verified hours, zone and reservable capacity. “Searching” is not an assignment or arrival promise. Scheduled bookings use a declared timezone and explicit requested window; whether capacity is reserved in advance or dispatch activates later is OPEN. Never sell a confirmed slot while merely recording interest. Server clock decides expiry; fanout, TTL, radius, service durations and retry policy are OPEN configurable proposals, not invented numeric defaults. Changes invalidate stale generations.

**Cancellation/no-show/dispute:** before assignment, cancel atomically against accept. After assignment, cancellation releases only the correct reservation once and records stage/reason/policy. Reassignment creates a new generation after prior release. Post-arrival or unsafe/in-progress work needs a separately approved safety/exit policy; do not automatically cancel to hide incidents. No-show timing and appeals require actual staffed operations and approval. Complaints retain evidence and negative outcomes without publishing personal data. Reviews require genuine eligible service interaction, one reviewer scope and moderation provenance; no paid suppression.

**Notifications:** backend state commits before enqueue. Event ID, request generation/version, recipient, purpose, language, template version, expiry, dedupe key and provider result are distinct. Minimize payload and hide exact address until authorized. Track queued/attempted/provider-accepted/delivered/read only where actually supported; none means booking acceptance. Retry bounded by relevance/TTL and current consent; expired offers never remain actionable. Opt-out respects purpose and channel while retaining lawful service records. Official WhatsApp template/window/rate/account constraints remain P01-DOD-008 blockers; no fallback to unofficial automation. FCM/APNs/Web Push are pending account/device permission and implementation validation.

**Localization and accessibility:** persist English/Telugu preference across explicitly linked channels. Separate UI language, customer quote text and merchant free text; never present unreviewed machine translation as agreed contractual scope. Dates show local zone where ambiguity matters; server stores instants plus requested timezone. Provide keyboard, screen-reader, non-color status, clear field errors and accessible support paths. Content and font licenses, Telugu text shaping and native-language review remain pending.

**Privacy and operations:** no public request/photo indexing, precise customer address in pre-acceptance offers or generic analytics export. Private uploads need size/type checks, quarantine/scanning, object-level authorization, signed short-lived access, metadata stripping and retention. Exact limits, locations and periods await security/legal approval. Account deletion must address linked channel identifiers, media, legal holds, immutable minimized audit and restore tombstones. No serious incident waits for a speculative support-hours promise; launch requires an actual staffed escalation route.

**Customer-facing help and legal information:** reserve discoverable Support, Contact, Terms and Conditions, and applicable privacy/policy entry points across the approved customer channels. Support routes must state actual availability and escalation boundaries, not imply unstaffed coverage. Terms and policies require qualified legal review, versioning, language review, and affirmative acceptance where required; publishing placeholder text is not legal clearance. Account deletion remains a privacy-rights workflow with lawful holds and a truthful completion receipt, not an unconditional hard-delete promise.

**Location and navigation:** support explicit current-location permission where available and manual address/pin entry when permission is denied or inaccurate. Google location/maps services are a candidate provider only; key ownership, billing, terms, retention and accuracy must be verified before selection. Share precise directions with a provider only after an authorized assignment and only for the service purpose; do not expose continuous customer or technician tracking by default. Merchant navigation may open an approved map/directions destination without changing canonical booking state.

**Provider and implementation choices:** official WhatsApp from an authorized Fix Hub sender and Twilio as a possible BSP, plus Resend for transactional email, are requested candidates—not selected or configured integrations. Confirm account eligibility, sender/domain ownership, consent/templates, data-processing terms, cost, webhook security, retries and provider-failure behavior before implementation. Pino, Helmet, CORS policy, Express rate limiting, input-validation libraries, and Swagger/OpenAPI are candidate engineering mechanisms; the backend framework and exact package choices remain subject to the approved architecture. Regardless of stack, require structured redacted logs, case-scoped diagnostics, explicit origin policy, endpoint-specific abuse limits, schema validation and a versioned API contract. Never log credentials, message bodies, photos, precise location or unrestricted personal data.

**Interaction quality:** loading, empty, error and offline states must be fast, truthful and recoverable; motion is nonessential, brief, and respects reduced-motion settings. Establish measurable performance budgets, device/network test profiles and usability acceptance criteria before build. “Premium,” “easy login,” and uniquely superior are aspirations, not test evidence or comparative claims; validate them through accessible task completion and user testing rather than decoration or unsupported marketing.

Rights workflow candidate: receive a scoped request; verify authority without collecting excess documents; identify affected systems/processors; allow correction/access/export/deletion/withdrawal only where legally applicable; record hold/restriction decisions; obtain required processor acknowledgements; reconcile backups with tombstones; issue a truthful partial/complete/denied outcome and retain a minimized audit receipt. Applicable rights, identity assurance, processor duties, deadlines and retention exceptions remain legal decisions; no deadline or universal deletion promise is implied.

Upload contract candidate: reject unsupported or mismatched content types and active/executable formats; enforce owner-approved byte, count, dimension and decompression limits; store in quarantine outside public paths; scan in an isolated bounded process; fail closed on scan error; strip location metadata when not required; serve only after object-level authorization with short-lived access; expire temporary derivatives and record cleanup. Exact allowlists/vendor controls and malicious-file tests remain pending; no upload control is implemented by this specification.

Session and privileged-access contract candidate: define authentication/recovery, OTP attempt throttles, anti-automation, session expiry/revocation, device lifecycle, number recycling and stronger assurance for privileged actions before implementation. Require deny-path tests for revoked sessions, cross-tenant objects, self-grant, reviewer conflicts and role/field masking. Encryption at rest/in transit, key ownership/rotation, tamper-evident audit and recovery behavior must be specified with the actual provider/security design; no deployment control or certification is claimed.
