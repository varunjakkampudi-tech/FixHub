## In scope for the proposed launch product

### Customer experience

- Discover local service providers such as electricians, AC repair technicians, plumbers, and approved future categories.
- Search by service, category, requirement, and location.
- Submit instant or scheduled requests through WhatsApp, website, Android, or iOS.
- Provide address, service details, and optional photos.
- View truthful request-searching and merchant-assignment states.
- Review merchant information and applicable pricing when verified and available.
- Track progress, cancel within defined rules, review, and complain.
- Use English or Telugu.

### Merchant experience

- Register without a registration fee.
- Complete verification requirements.
- Maintain services, prices, availability, coverage, and technician capacity.
- Receive eligible offers on WhatsApp, website dashboard, Android, and iOS.
- Accept or decline offers.
- Receive assignment only after an authoritative backend first-valid-acceptance decision.
- Manage jobs, technicians, completion, reviews, and support.

### Super-admin and operations

- Securely manage customers, merchants, categories, cities, and service zones.
- Approve, reject, suspend, and verify merchants.
- Monitor requests and dispatch outcomes.
- Investigate cancellations, disputes, fraud, and incidents.
- Manage staff roles, permissions, notification policies, and dispatch configuration.
- Review analytics, platform costs, audit logs, and system health.

## Cross-channel and platform scope

- One authoritative backend for WhatsApp, website, Android, and iOS.
- Real-time status where technically and operationally verified.
- Authorized WhatsApp Cloud API integration subject to provider and compliance validation.
- App Store and Play Store publication as objectives subject to approval.

## Explicit exclusions from Phase 00 and unapproved scope

- Permanent logo, color palette, typography, visual identity, tagline, domain, or brand system.
- Final UI screens or design implementation.
- Lovable project creation or assumed repository import/synchronization.
- Payment processing, refunds, subscriptions, commissions, liability coverage, insurance, or platform guarantees.
- Claims of guaranteed provider availability, price, response time, or arrival time.
- Public launch, deployment, production database, cloud infrastructure, or app-store submission.
- Expansion beyond Tanuku.
- Paid advertisements or paid ranking preference.

## Scope boundaries requiring later decisions

- Service category taxonomy and launch category count.
- Customer and merchant pricing disclosure rules.
- Cancellation, dispute, refund, and complaint policy.
- Merchant verification evidence and re-verification cadence.
- Geographic service-zone definition and location precision.
- Supported WhatsApp flows and template approval.
- Native-app versus cross-platform delivery approach.
- Staffing, support hours, service-level targets, and launch capacity.

## Scope guardrails

Every later scope addition requires an identifier, owner, acceptance criteria, cost/risk impact, and explicit phase approval. No proposed architecture or tool choice in this document is a build authorization.
