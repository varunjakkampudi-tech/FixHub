# Customer interview guide

How-to instrument; NOT EXECUTED. Use [consent](customer-consent-template.md); record anonymous ID, date, zone bucket, adult consent, interview language, recruitment route and interviewer. Do not record public names/phones/addresses.

## Open with recent behavior

1. Tell me about the most recent time you needed an electrician, plumber, AC or appliance repairer. When and what happened?
2. How did you find them? What alternatives did you actually try?
3. How long until contact, acceptance and arrival? Which timestamps are recalled rather than observed?
4. What did you pay for inspection, labour, parts and travel? Was anything agreed before the visit?
5. What worked well? What went wrong? How was it resolved?
6. How many such jobs can you recall in the last12 months? Which are uncertain?
7. Did you reuse a known provider? Why stay with or switch from them?
8. Was this urgent or schedulable? What delay would have been acceptable?

## Preferences grounded in trade-offs

9. Which channel did you actually use: call, WhatsApp, web, app or referral? What prevented another?
10. What evidence would make an unfamiliar provider trustworthy? What would not?
11. How would inspection cost affect seeking a quote? Ask their amount first, not our proposed price.
12. What cancellation, no-show or dispute remedy would they expect, and what did the last provider do?
13. Who in the household decides, pays and grants entry? Do not collect their identifiers.
14. Which services would they refuse to book remotely? Why?

Only then neutrally describe a proposed local matching service with free merchant registration, uncertain fees and no guaranteed availability. Ask what would stop switching, what they would try first, what information they need and what evidence would change their mind. Never ask only whether they like the idea.

## Verification and troubleshooting

Read back key facts; distinguish receipts seen from memory without copying receipts. Participant may skip/stop. If consent uncertain, stop and exclude notes. If no recent job, record no-case rather than invent a case. Close without sales promises, consent to future marketing or onboarding. Link coded findings to [results](customer-results.md).
