# Customer and merchant research consent template

DRAFT, not yet counsel/language-reviewed or used. Owner must supply real researcher/contact/withdrawal route privately before recruitment. Blank fields are intentionally unfilled; do not present this as executed consent.

Study: FixHub24 feasibility under Viswatra; proposed service matching in Tanuku. Interview is voluntary and takes approximately 20–30 minutes. No service, merchant contract or employment is offered. Choosing not to participate has no penalty. You may skip questions or stop.

We propose collecting anonymous age-adult confirmation, broad zone/household or business category, recent service behavior, costs and opinions. Do not provide government IDs, exact home address, bank details or other people's personal information. No recording unless separately explained and affirmatively accepted. Consent to research is not consent to marketing or WhatsApp notifications.

Proposed use: aggregate themes and anonymized counts to decide whether further planning is viable. Anonymous summaries may enter a public repository; raw notes and contact records will not. Even a quote may identify someone locally, so default to paraphrasing and remove unique details. Proposed90 day raw-note retention after analysis requires approval; explain actual deletion/withdrawal limits before collecting. Withdrawal cannot remove an already irreversibly anonymized aggregate.

Researcher name/authority: privately supply. Research and withdrawal contact: privately supply a working route. Language chosen: record. Questions answered: record. Adult voluntary consent: YES/NO, date and instrument version. Notes permission: YES/NO. Recording: NOT REQUESTED by default. Recontact: NOT REQUESTED by default. Incentive and conditions: NONE unless separately approved and disclosed.

If contact/retention/language safeguards are not ready, do not recruit. Merchant interviews use this same voluntary notice plus business-sensitive information exclusions. See [data map](../legal/privacy-and-data-map.md).
