# Customer research plan

Status: READY instrument; PRIMARY VALIDATION PENDING. Target25–30 consenting adults; completed0. Owner: research lead, human owner authorizes recruitment and data collection. No outreach authority exercised. See [guide](customer-interview-guide.md), [consent](customer-consent-template.md), [results](customer-results.md).

## Recruitment and sampling

Aim30: five provisional neighborhood strata × six interviews, distributed across owners/renters, independent homes/apartments, younger/older adult decision makers, digitally comfortable/offline-reliant households. Select actual zones after boundary verification. Avoid recruiting only friends or WhatsApp-heavy users; track recruitment source and refusals without identities. Exclude minors and people unable to give informed consent. Convenience quotas are not representative sampling.

Owner approves recruitment script, channel, language, incentives and private storage first. Offer Telugu/English consent and interview; language review is pending. Research is not a booking, employment offer or guaranteed benefit. Incentives if later approved must not depend on favorable answers.

## Method and safeguards

Interviews take 20–30 minutes, with notes by default; no audio, photos, precise address, phone or ID without separate justified consent. Ask about recent behavior before revealing Fix Hub. Use anonymous C01-001-style identifiers; store recruitment contact keys separately with restricted access, never in public Git. Proposed raw-note retention is 90 days after analysis, with withdrawal before aggregation through a privately supplied contact; the legal retention basis is not yet approved.

Two-pass thematic coding: recent case, source, costs, time, failure, workaround, switch barriers, channel, quote tolerance. Preserve negative cases and missing responses; counts always include denominator. Compare strata, do not report inferential town prevalence or confidence intervals from convenience interviews.

## Thresholds fixed before data

Demand screen: >=20 usable recent service cases, >=10 clearly documented unresolved discovery/reliability/price problems and >=10 independently expressed willingness to try a neutrally described proposition. All are count-based research signals, not market validation alone. Category screens in [shortlist](../market/service-category-opportunities.md) also apply. Reject/revise if problems are rare, current relationships satisfy needs or lawful price exceeds willingness. Stated interest alone does not pass P01-DOD-003.

No field trial is authorized. Record authorization/date/instrument version first; report analyzed sample, saturation, contradictions and selection bias before any recommendation.
