# Tanuku market assessment

Status: CONDITIONAL desk assessment; current demand and catchment NOT ASSESSED. Owner: delegated product/research lead. Sources/retrieval/freshness: [source register](../evidence/source-register.md), SRC-001–005.

## What the sources support

The municipal report's tables give 2011 households22,607 and population90,430, but narrative census-year labels and ward numbers conflict. These are historical counts, not2026 estimates. The current municipal page supplies no retrievable numeric household count. Independent Census row retrieval remains pending. Do not substitute national smartphone adoption for town-level reachable households.

## Market-sizing method

Do not publish a numeric current TAM/SAM/SOM. TAM requires dated in-boundary households × observed category frequency × observed job value. SAM then filters serviceable zones, safe work types, verified provider coverage and supported channels. SOM is bounded by funded acquisition and demonstrated technician capacity, not an arbitrary share of TAM.

Illustrative finance request volumes400/1,200/3,000 per month are class5 assumptions, not forecasts. Municipal limits and travel catchment must be mapped separately; nearby village directory results cannot expand the market silently. Record zone definitions without storing precise residential locations publicly.

## Research questions and falsification

Measure recent actual electrical/plumbing/AC/appliance work, service frequency, paid inspection/parts/labour, failed searches, quality, repeat providers, urgent versus scheduled needs and switching barriers. Neighborhood, tenure, dwelling type and digital access are stratification questions, not established demographic findings.

Hypotheses: offline referrals may dominate trust; clearer prices and accountable local support may help; humid/hot periods may change AC demand. None is a verified Tanuku finding. No current seasonal job series or WhatsApp adoption estimate was found. Test these in [customer plan](../research/customer-research-plan.md) and [merchant plan](../research/merchant-research-plan.md).

## Recommendation

Research municipal Tanuku first; do not approve expanded catchment or launch commitments until demand, route times and legal provider coverage are observed. Missing current household data weakens market-size precision but does not justify invented growth.
