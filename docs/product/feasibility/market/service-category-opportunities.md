## Bounded recommendation and rationale

Recommend Tanuku-town electrical fault diagnosis/minor repair and plumbing diagnosis/minor repair as joint first research priorities. Neither outranks the other on measured demand or margin: no such evidence exists. Their narrow household fault use cases permit clearer service descriptions and inspection-price disclosure than an undifferentiated appliance catalogue. Electrical shock/fire and water/property-damage hazards make both contingent on task-specific competence and explicit exclusions, not inherently safe or low-cost categories. No local price is inferred from generic directory FAQs.

Retain AC servicing/repair as a second research track and appliance repair as a third, separated by appliance/OEM and repair type. AC seasonality, refrigerant competence, warranty restrictions, replacement parts and repeat-visit economics remain unmeasured. Do not collapse refrigerator, washing-machine and all appliance repair into one interchangeable technician pool. No commercial category is approved merely because a public listing exists.

For requirements drafting only, propose these four families as configurable taxonomy candidates, disabled for live service until actual coverage is demonstrated. Proposed initial scope excludes electrical utility/high-voltage work, unsafe live electrical work, structural/major plumbing, gas work, emergency/rescue promises, and OEM/warranty-authorized claims without proof. The precise lawful task boundaries need qualified review; this exclusion list is not a safety certification.

## Mandatory private-pilot and prelaunch evidence (not Phase 01 interview criteria)

Before offering actual services, verify independent willing merchants and competence per category/zone, realistic technician capacity, coverage, transparent service prices and credible real customer demand or pilot outcomes. Test resilience when one provider is unavailable. The previous interview-number screens remain historical optional research material, not Phase 01 completion requirements.

Compare price/inspection clarity, reliable arrival, acceptable coverage, technician safety and contribution under conservative assumptions. Do not add categories merely to make an A–Z name promise. Final launch category selection remains conditional on actual safe supply/market evidence, legal exclusions and explicit owner approval.
