# Merchant operating model

Status: proposed; unstaffed and not launch-ready. [Supply research](../research/merchant-research-plan.md) and [verification](verification-and-safety.md) are dependencies.

Free basic registration/listing remains. Owner-approved outreach explains eligibility, no promised jobs, possible future transparent fees and appeal rights; consent and business verification precede activation. Operations records category/zone/hours, lawful competence scope, independent technicians, capacity overlap and training completion. No live onboarding done.

Proposed lifecycle: application → private scoped review → corrections/decision with reason → activation only for valid tasks → expiry/re-verification → suspension/revocation/appeal. Technician roster changes require checks; suspended providers must fail backend acceptance even if cached listings persist. Separate applicant submission and approval duties where staffing permits; conflicts logged.

Founder may coordinate interviews, training and daytime queue review after authority; cannot honestly claim24/7 coverage, sole independent competence verification or legal adjudication. Technical trades, fraud escalation, legal advice and coverage backup need qualified people/arrangements.

Measure onboarding minutes, corrections, rejection/appeal rates, support cost, fee understanding, inactive providers and retention. Modelled merchant acquisition/verification costs are allowances, not quotes. Prevent repeated signup abuse without unnecessary ID collection. No merchant contract, employment relationship, fee or guarantee approved.
