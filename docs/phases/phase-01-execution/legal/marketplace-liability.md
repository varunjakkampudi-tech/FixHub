# Marketplace role and liability

Status: UNRESOLVED counsel assessment, not a drafted enforceable contract. The proposed marketplace does not automatically become legally insulated by calling itself an intermediary. Actual contracting, price control, supervision, payment, guarantee, dispute handling and representations matter.

Compare three legal/commercial structures: disclosed matching-only service with merchant contract; agent collecting/arranging service; principal service provider undertaking delivery. Each changes tax, consumer obligations, worker control, insurance and remedies. No structure approved; payment/refund/guarantee commitments excluded until counsel and owner decide.

Do not advertise verified as universally safe, guaranteed arrival, insured, background-checked or authorized OEM without evidence of exact scope/date. Verification may reduce specific uncertainty, never removes customer/site risk. Customer and merchant need transparent inspection/parts/travel terms, cancellations, grievance route and who contracts for the work.

Reviews/support may create moderation/record obligations; provider disputes require fair evidence access and appeals. Disclaimers cannot replace statutory consumer rights or prevent lawful incident reporting. Determine negligent referral/verification exposure, unsafe task exclusions, property damage, injury and emergency escalation with Indian counsel. No unsupported liability cap or insurance coverage proposed as fact.
