# Lawful merchant verification boundary

Status: proposed risk-based process, no merchant verified. Human/counsel approve documents and lawful basis before collection.

Start with business/task identity, self-declared category, permitted competence evidence and independent references obtained only after consent/authority. Distinguish business ownership, technician identity, technical competence, trade permission and insurance; passing one does not prove the others. Use evidence status/scope/expiry, not a universal verified badge.

No Aadhaar, government-ID image, face biometrics, criminal database check or private contact harvest authorized. Ask counsel what least-data alternative establishes required identity, whether any task has mandatory credentials and when third-party check is lawful. Retain result/reference rather than a raw document where sufficient; restrict reviewers and keep private data out of Git.

Assign verifier/reviewer conflicts, rejection reasons, appeal route, expiry, re-verification, technician roster changes and revocation. A suspended/unverified provider must be ineligible at acceptance commit, not just hidden from search. Scope exclusions cover live electrical hazards, unsupported specialist/warranty work and unsafe access until policy approved.

Estimate verification time/cost per genuine new business and avoid counting the same technician in several merchants. [Operations](../operations/verification-and-safety.md), [merchant plan](../research/merchant-research-plan.md).
