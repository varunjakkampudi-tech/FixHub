# Business model options

Status: alternatives assessed; monetization NOT APPROVED. Free basic merchant registration/listing remains unchanged. Paid ranking/advertisements are excluded. Payment processing, refund guarantees and liability undertakings remain unapproved.

| Alternative | Value and fairness test | Revenue proof needed | Risk / provisional position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completed-job fee/commission | Charge only on documented completion; transparent inspection/parts exclusions | Merchant acceptance, lawful collection and audit/dispute process | Modelled₹60/120/150 are assumptions; bypass/cash leakage and tax classification unresolved |
| Accepted lead fee | Merchant consents to charge and definition of qualified lead | Invalid/duplicate/unavailable lead credit rules | Charging for failed matches can destroy trust; no fee approval |
| Optional business tools subscription | Basic listing remains free; genuinely optional extra value | Recurring willingness, retention, store digital-purchase policy | No paid ranking; early supply may not value tools |
| Customer convenience fee | Clear total before request; affordable alternative | Actual willingness and counsel/store review | Could depress conversion; no approved fee |
| Transparent enterprise/service partnership | Contracted value, no hidden placement | Independent commercial/operational evidence | No partnerships/terms exist |

Initial research comparison favors completed-job evidence over charging all leads, but no commercial launch choice is made. Merchant direct payment does not prove platform fee collection or remove GST/ECO obligations. No fee revenue is realized today.

Use [unit economics](unit-economics.md), [legal questions](../legal/legal-counsel-questions.md) and interview fee trade-offs before approval. Reject a model if conservatively observed revenue fails contribution, acquisition recovery and truthful service promises.
