## Differentiation to test, not approve

Test timestamped acceptance, visible inspection/labour/parts terms, support accountability, verified identity scope and continuation from WhatsApp to web/app. Customers may prefer a familiar technician even if these features exist. Interview negative switchers and providers who reject fees, not just enthusiasts.

Prioritize substitution evidence over a fabricated feature score. A later comparison should record address/pincode coverage, quoted final price, cancellations, grievance path and observed delivery only with approved test/outreach authority. No competitor market-share percentage is justified.
