# Support and dispute feasibility

Status: proposed; service hours, personnel and binding remedies unapproved. No public service launched. Sources legal framework in [compliance register](../legal/india-compliance-register.md); operational targets below proposals, not delivered SLAs.

Create one case per job with category, severity, contact preference and scoped evidence. Acknowledge with current status, not guaranteed resolution. Handle missing assignment/no-show by checking committed state and contacting through consented routes; reassignment requires safe cancellation/generation and capacity release, never second simultaneous winner.

Safety risk takes precedence: stop unsafe matching, advise appropriate local emergency route without claiming emergency-service capability, preserve minimal evidence and escalate to designated human. Cyber/privacy cases need security lead/CERT-In applicability assessment; complaints about money/damage require authorized legal/operations review.

Proposed staffed window09:00–18:00, six days/week; outside-window notices clearly say unstaffed. This window is an assumption, not owner-approved availability. Serious incident coverage requires named backup/on-call, otherwise no launch. Where applicable consumer rules require48 h acknowledgement/one-month redress, and staffing must actually meet them, including absence/weekends.

Complaint process: record allegation → mask unrelated data → allow merchant/customer response → fair evidence review → authorized decision/reason → appeal/review. No automatic refund, warranty or payment authority. Fraud/abuse reports do not publicly convict providers. Suppress reviews only under transparent policy, not because negative.

Case metrics: arrivals/no-shows, first human response, ageing, repeat visits, disputed fees, appeals and recurrence. Retention/legal holds and evidence access await legal policy. [Capacity](capacity-and-staffing.md).
