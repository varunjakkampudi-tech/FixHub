# Merchant interview guide

How-to instrument; NOT EXECUTED. Start with voluntary adult consent and anonymous ID; explain research, not onboarding or guaranteed leads. [Plan](merchant-research-plan.md).

1. What categories and tasks do you actually perform? Which do you refuse?
2. Walk through last week's jobs, travel, inspections, job durations and cancellations without customer identifiers.
3. How many technicians work with you? Which also work elsewhere? How are assignments tracked?
4. Which zones and hours can you genuinely cover? What affects travel and after-hours safety?
5. What makes you accept or decline a job? What response window is realistic during work?
6. How do you reserve future slots, avoid overbooking and handle emergency overruns?
7. What inspection, labour, parts and travel charges do you disclose? What makes an estimate change?
8. How do no-shows, callbacks, warranties, returns and disputes work today?
9. What competence evidence or references would you voluntarily permit verification of? What data would you refuse to provide?
10. How do you communicate with customers? Can you reliably use a web dashboard/app on your own device?
11. Would free basic listing matter if demand were uncertain? What onboarding burden is unacceptable?
12. Compare transparent completed-job fees, accepted-lead fees and optional subscriptions without implying an approved model. Ask acceptable amount, billing proof and dispute terms first.
13. What would cause you to leave after one month? How much support/training is needed?
14. What do existing discovery platforms or referrals do better?
15. Explain a difficult safety or quality case without identifying a person.

Probe actual recent behavior, not only stated interest. Separate business count from technician count and willingness from verified competence, device capability or signed availability. Do not photograph certificates, collect Aadhaar or make commitments. Read back capacity/fee understanding; stop if consent uncertain.

Future results include negative providers and missing data. [Results](merchant-results.md), [verification boundary](../legal/merchant-verification.md).
