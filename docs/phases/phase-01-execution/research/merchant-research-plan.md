# Merchant research plan

Status: READY instrument; supply validation PENDING. Target15–20 consented independent technicians/small businesses across four categories; actual0. Owner: research/operations lead, human authorizes outreach. No live onboarding.

Aim20 across four categories, independent/small-business mix and >=3 municipal zone buckets. Deduplicate businesses that list several categories; one organization must not count as independent redundancy three times. Interview refusals and unwilling providers to avoid enthusiasm bias. Public listings are discovery pointers only, not verified participants.

Use [consent](customer-consent-template.md) and [guide](merchant-interview-guide.md). Do not collect identity documents, customer names, private contacts, certificates or bank details in Git. Record anonymized category, zone, staffing ranges, availability, last-week actual completed work and price/fee views. Owner supplies approved private evidence storage and outreach identity first.

## Precommitted screens and analysis

Require >=15 usable interviews with recent workload examples; per candidate initial category >=3 independently willing businesses with assessed competence/coverage and capacity surviving one unavailable provider per zone. Willingness must include explicit understanding of free registration, possible transparent fees, verification and no guaranteed leads. It is not a signed commitment.

Capture hourly availability, travel/inspection/job duration, parallel jobs, technician overlap, declined requests, cancellation/no-show causes, invoicing and dispute practices. Evaluate dashboard/WhatsApp capability on their actual device only with authorized consent later. Stated daily capacity must be reconciled against observed prior-week jobs and other work.

Report contradictions between interest and fee/verification willingness, single-point categories/zones, unverified claims and seasonal constraints. Use [supply model](merchant-supply-model.md) only as a hypothesis until measured. Revise or stop if lawful qualified supply is insufficient, providers reject transparent fees or customer promise cannot be staffed. No merchant agreement or placement promise is authorized.
